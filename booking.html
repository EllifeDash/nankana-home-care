Questions?

Frequently Asked

How quickly can someone arrive?

For same-day bookings placed before 6pm, we typically dispatch within 1–3 hours depending on attendant availability in your area.

Do you bring all equipment?

Yes. Our attendants carry everything needed — sterile syringes, dressing materials, glucometer, BP cuff, and PPE. You don't need to buy anything.

How is payment handled?

Cash payment after the visit is completed. Easypaisa and bank transfer are also accepted. A receipt is always provided.

What if I need to cancel?

Please notify us at least 1 hour before your appointment via phone or WhatsApp. We are flexible and will reschedule at no extra charge.
