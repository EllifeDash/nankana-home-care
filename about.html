Our Beginning

Started from a Family Need, Grown into a Community Service

Nankana Home Care was founded after our team witnessed first-hand the difficulty families face transporting sick or elderly patients to hospitals repeatedly for routine procedures. We saw injections, blood draws, and wound dressings turning into stressful, costly, and time-consuming hospital trips.

We started small — one medical attendant, one neighbourhood. Today, we serve hundreds of families across Nankana Sahib, Sangla Hill, and surrounding areas with a team of certified professionals and a growing service portfolio.

Our mission is simple: bring clinical-grade care to where patients are most comfortable — home.
